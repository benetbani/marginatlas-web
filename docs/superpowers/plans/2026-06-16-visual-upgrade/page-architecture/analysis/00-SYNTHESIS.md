## Site-wide section taxonomy (the redundancy map)

Grouping every section across all six page types by the **job** it does. Eight jobs recur on more than one page type; each needs one canonical owner and a defined deferral for the rest. This is where cross-page slop dies.

**JOB A: The money split / "what the owner keeps" (per-$100 or take-home).**
Appears on: cell (#4 + #6), industry (#4 + #7), city (#6), country (proposed new take-home), home (tiles #2, newsletter preview #9, state comparison #4).
- **OWNER: Cell** (#4 "Where the money goes"). The cell is the only entity with a real, full, place-specific P&L; it is the canonical home of the per-$100 split and the take-home number.
- **Industry** keeps it as a *place-stable structural shape* (the flow diagram = the story, the per-$100 bar = the snapshot, the table = the accounting) and must NOT also restate it a fourth time as re-ranked cost drivers (cut its standalone cost-drivers section).
- **City** carries it only as a *menu* across trades (ranked take-home with a cell link), never a single deep split.
- **Country** gets at most ONE illustrative/structural waterfall framed as such, hard-linked to the cell for the live number.
- **Home** picks ONE canonical "what the owner keeps" visual and forces the example tiles + state comparison to vary their metric (revenue, margin), so the front door teaches several numbers, not the same one thrice.
- **RULE:** depth flows downward only. Country/industry show the *shape*; the city shows the *menu*; the cell shows the *dollars*. No page above the cell may print a take-home dollar figure as if it owned it.

**JOB B: How-hard / the score / break-in difficulty.**
Appears on: cell (break-in ScoreBand, vs-world), city (Business Climate Score), country (scorecard reads, vs-world), neighbourhood (relative multiplier, explicitly never scored).
- **OWNER: City** (Business Climate Score). Per the constitution, **cities are the ONLY scored entity** — the city page owns the 0-100 verdict outright.
- **Cell** keeps a *break-in* difficulty band (a trade-in-place readiness read, anchored to a stated peer set), which is a different question from the city's market score and is allowed.
- **Country** must NOT score — its scorecard reads are reframed "high vs the global baseline, never a verdict," and the rejected radar (a composite that reads AS a score) is killed.
- **Neighbourhood** never scores; it shows a *relative multiplier* vs its own city only.
- **RULE:** one scored entity (city). Everyone else gives a located, captioned, non-aggregate read. "Versus the world" collapses to the calm strip until a real worldwide read is held, and on the cell, where it rarely holds, demote it to the industry page.

**JOB C: Like-for-like peers / "compared to what."**
Appears on: cell (same-business-nearby), city (peer cities), country (vs-neighbours), industry (US states), neighbourhood (vs-next-door), home (state comparison).
- **OWNER: depends on the comparison axis** — and the constitution's "never rank across business x geography" forces a clean split:
  - *Same trade, across PLACES* -> **City** owns city-vs-city (the one legitimate ranking, identical scale); **cell** owns same-trade-nearby (anti-ranking framing); **neighbourhood** owns district-vs-next-door (one-city leader mark allowed).
  - *Same trade, across JURISDICTIONS* -> **Country** owns the vs-neighbours facts table (no crown, home column tinted).
  - *Same trade, across US states* -> **Industry** owns the ranked states bar (one currency, one tax system).
- **Home** carries ONE capability demo (reformed to take-home, not revenue) and defers the rest.
- **RULE:** the country page's "same business here vs abroad" is **cut** — it is literally Compare's job; route its value to the Compare CTA. No page invents a second peer visual that duplicates its primary one (city: cut the VsWorld median strip; the ranked bars already locate the subject).

**JOB D: The honest take / editorial verdict.**
Appears on EVERY page type, plus a "one thing to remember" closer on each, plus on the cell a third restatement in "in context."
- **OWNER: each page owns its own**, but every page must enforce the same discipline: the honest-take = *current watch-outs / diagnosis*; the one-thing closer = *forward-looking, actionable instruction*. They must say **escalating** things, not the same thing twice.
- **RULE (the strictest cross-page cut):** the cell's "in context" is a *third* restatement of the verdict and is **cut/repurposed**. On industry and country, audit that the thesis, the honest-take, and the closer are three distinct registers. A verdict said three times before the closer is the clearest editorial slop on the site.

**JOB E: "Can I survive the slow start?" (ramp / runway / time-to-breakeven).**
Appears on: cell (break-even, first year, present and strong), and is **MISSING** on city, industry, country, and neighbourhood, yet all four audits flag it as the persona's single most-requested gap.
- **OWNER: Cell** (it has the gauge + the timeline ribbon, the real survival instruments).
- **RULE:** add a *modeled, honestly-tagged* ramp/runway beat to industry (months-to-breakeven line), city (capital-to-open + months-to-break-even), and neighbourhood (a qualitative week-shape/season strip). The home page gets only a teaser hook. This is the highest-frequency missing job across the whole site: four of six audits independently demand it.

**JOB F: Who the customer is / demand.**
Appears on: city (income spread, visitor split), neighbourhood (who-lives-here), and is a **MISSING** trade-specific slice on the cell ("who eats here") and a re-derived thin read on country (who-has-money).
- **OWNER: City** (the income-spread RangeStrip is the canonical customer-wallet read).
- **RULE:** city fuses its three thin market sections (income / cost / visitors) into ONE "your customer" band. The cell adds a *trade-specific* day-part slice (who eats here), not a generic city read. Country's "who has money" is **merged into the scorecard** as a derived spending-power tile, not a standalone horoscope word.

**JOB G: Cost structure / cost drivers / "what eats the margin."**
Appears on: cell (cost drivers, redundant with the money split), industry (cost drivers, admits "no new numbers"), country (cost signature, sample).
- **OWNER: Cell + Industry's per-$100 split (Job A) already carry this.** The standalone "ranked cost drivers" section is **redundant on every page it appears.**
- **RULE:** cut the standalone re-rank everywhere. Replace it ONLY where it can become genuinely new: a *sensitivity* view (cell: "which lever, moved 10%, hurts most") or a *variance* spread (industry: "how much each line varies good-vs-bad operator"). Country's cost-signature folds into the strip until real.

**JOB H: Wayfinding / hand-off / related.**
Appears on: every page (navbar, footer, related tiles), and over-proliferates on neighbourhood (three district-pointing sections: adjacent, businesses-here, related) and city (best-areas + neighbourhoods, both about districts).
- **OWNER: one closing hand-off zone per page.**
- **RULE:** neighbourhood merges `businesses-here` + `related` into one "Where to look next." City merges best-areas + neighbourhoods into one "Where to set up." Keep the *compared* sibling table separate from the *browse* rail (different jobs), but never three link grids in a row.

## The reinventions (deep)

**1. Country "nine-lens shape" (radar rejected).**
- **Why it fails the customer:** a nine-spoke polygon reads as a single composite *score*, the exact thing the constitution forbids for a country, and two of nine spokes are admitted SAMPLE, so the shape is literally dented by missing data. A P1 cannot act on "Edge: Strong." It also heavily overlaps the scorecard (Reward/Cost/Entry/People restate GDP/CoL/days/wages).
- **Alternatives:**
  - *(a) Seven-row strength matrix* (categorical rungs weak to excellent, the two sample lenses removed): loses the "one shape" elegance, but kills the score-read and the placeholder ghosts.
  - *(b) Kill it entirely, fold real content into the scorecard:* cleanest de-dup, but loses the qualitative character beat that humanizes the page.
  - *(c) Keep a radar but only on fully-held countries:* perpetuates a banned composite-score read; rejected.
- **RECOMMENDATION: (a) the seven-row discrete strength matrix**, sample lenses cut, audited against the scorecard so the two cards say genuinely different things. The exact visual: a vertical list of seven labelled rows, each a 4-rung categorical band (weak / fair / strong / excellent) with a filled rung and a one-line "why", no aggregate, no polygon, no number.

**2. City "best areas to set up."**
- **Why it fails the customer:** as mocked it is a bare district/suits/why list, generic local-guide copy ("Soho is trendy and expensive") with no trade-off data, the most *actionable* section on the page left underbuilt. It also collides with the neighbourhoods rail (both are "districts").
- **Alternatives:**
  - *(a) Suits-shortlist + rent/footfall mini-meters:* adds the real decision input (rent-vs-footfall trade-off as a scan) and absorbs the neighbourhoods rail as the links; the meters must be labelled "relative within this city."
  - *(b) Heat-grid matrix:* denser but reads as a fabricated-precision scorecard and cramps at 375px; rejected.
  - *(c) Card grid:* clones the other rails and violates no-repeated-grids; rejected.
- **RECOMMENDATION: (a)**, merged with the neighbourhoods rail. The exact visual: a divided list, one row per district, each carrying a suits-pictogram + a 4-segment rent pip + a 4-segment footfall pip (relative within the city), the row itself linking to that neighbourhood page, with an "Explore all neighbourhoods" tail. One band does the decision job AND the navigation job.

**3. The third reinvention found: Industry "how it makes money" + "the cost stack" (the mockup ships the superseded specs).**
This is the most consequential reinvention because the live mockup actively contradicts the founder's own ratified overrides.
- **Why the current form fails the customer:** the how-it-works ships a 2x2 grid of qualitative signal words (Light / High / Light / Thin), abstract adjectives that are the most "AI-made-that" object on the page, and the cost stack ships a vertical waterfall that is the *third* bar-shape showing the *same* canonical split as the per-$100 bar and the signal grid. Three views of one number = bar soup.
- **Alternatives:**
  - *(a) Flow-of-money diagram + table:* three shapes, three jobs, three personas: the flow diagram = the *story* of the shrink ($100 to $65 to $19 to $7, for P5/P1), the per-$100 bar = the *snapshot* (everyone), the table = the *precise accounting* (P3/P4). Capital is dropped out of the sale-flow (it is a starting condition, not a stage).
  - *(b) Keep the waterfall, cut the signal grid:* still two bar-narratives of the shrink; doesn't fully kill bar soup.
  - *(c) Sankey:* over-data-viz'd for a non-expert and overkill for four stages; rejected.
- **RECOMMENDATION: (a)** — implement the flow-of-money diagram for how-it-works and the gross/operating/net table for the cost stack. The single exact visual that matters: a left-to-right **flow-of-money diagram** with shrinking connectors (mobile-stacked), so the reader *watches* the sale deplete.

(Honorable mention, a fourth latent reinvention: the **cell donut**. The founder overrode "never a pie" to a donut for the money split; the audit rules it works against both the honesty rule and the data's depletion shape, and recommends reverting to the waterfall. Folded into the Top 12 below rather than treated as a full reinvention, since the better form already exists in the live HTML.)

## What this buys the customer

**P1 (the prospective owner, core).** Today the cell restates the take-home four times and never answers the question P1 actually loses sleep over: *can I survive the slow start, and would I make more taking a job?* The reform stops the restatement (cut "in context," cut faux voices, revert the donut to the honest waterfall) and reinvests those slots into the survival arc: a ramp/runway beat on every page, take-home reframed as opportunity-cost (per hour, vs a salary), and on the neighbourhood the squeeze (a 2x revenue lift is not a 2x profit lift, and the rent is the reason) made *visible* instead of left for P1 to subtract. P1 leaves with a survival decision, not a vanity revenue number.

**P2 (the buyer).** P2 is currently the worst-served persona, with no "what is this worth" anywhere on the flagship. The reform adds a cell-level exit/valuation beat (rooms like this change hands at about X times owner earnings), gives the take-home a denominator and a good-vs-bad spread so P2 can sanity-check an owner's add-back, and routes the cross-place benchmarking P2 needs (same trade, comparable places) to clean, anti-ranking comparators on the cell and neighbourhood. P2 gains the deal-sizing inputs the site does not offer today.

**P3 (the investor/lender/PE, a client, never a subject).** P3 will not cite a number they cannot defend, and today there is no "how we know" anywhere on the site. The reform adds the methodology/confidence layer P3 pays for (per-cell held-vs-modeled reads, a site-wide trust strip, freshness stamps), reframes every country read as "vs the global baseline, never a verdict" so the structural benchmarks (payroll on-cost, formation cost, tax burden) are citation-safe and like-for-like, and de-duplicates the comparison surfaces so each jurisdiction/state/city benchmark has one canonical, honest home. P3 gets a defensible, citable structural read instead of confident-but-unsourced figures.
