## The eight customer jobs and their owners (the redundancy law)

Every section must serve one of these jobs; if it can't name a job no sibling section does, it is cut or merged. Depth flows DOWNWARD only.

- **A. What the owner keeps (money split):** OWNER = Cell (real dollars). Industry = the structural shape; City = the menu across trades; Country = one illustrative waterfall, hard-linked to the cell. No page above the cell prints take-home as if it owned it.

- **E. Survive the slow start (ramp/runway):** OWNER = Cell. Add a modeled, tagged ramp beat to City, Industry, Neighbourhood; a teaser only on Home.
- **F. Who the customer is:** OWNER = City (income spread). Cell adds a trade-specific day-part slice.
- **G. Cost drivers:** folded into Job A. The standalone re-rank is cut everywhere; it returns only as a NEW sensitivity (cell) or variance (industry) view.
- **H. Wayfinding:** one closing hand-off zone per page. Never three link grids in a row.

## Four anti-slop rules (apply to every page)

1. **No restatement loops.** A number or a verdict is stated once at full weight; later mentions must add a new frame (per-hour, vs salary, vs peer), never repeat.
2. **No bar soup.** One canonical split per page gets at most: the story shape + the snapshot + the precise table, each a distinct job. Never three bar-narratives of the same number.
3. **No placeholder ghosts.** Unheld data collapses into ONE calm "still filling in" strip, never multiple sample cards posing as content, never a dented chart.
4. **No fake-precision / vanity.** Every section earns its place with a real number or a real decision. No no-number cards on a numbers product; no qualitative signal-word grids standing in for data.
