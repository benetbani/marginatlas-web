# City page — candidate sections

## The curated menu (grouped by theme)

### Movement 1 — The Verdict (at-a-glance, the page's hook)

**1. City Scorecard + Climate Score**
The one calibrated panel: local pay, rent, cost of living, demand depth, with the Business Climate Score as the page's signature. Cities are the only scored entity.
*Chart:* stat-card cluster (bento) with one gauge for the score · **FREE** · **MUST-ADD**

**2. What This City Wins and Loses At**
The honest decomposition of the score's drivers — high spending power but punishing rent, deep footfall but seasonal. Frames the whole page; makes the score arguable, not a black box. (Dedupes "What drives the score" + "Best/worst at" + "Verdict strip.")
*Chart:* diverging bar (per-driver deviation from typical city) · **FREE** · **MUST-ADD**

**3. Red Flags, Honestly**
The operating drag the brochures won't name — brutal seasonality, hollow weekends, oversupply, shrinking working-age base. Trust-builder, each flag grounded in a real figure on the page.
*Chart:* simple table with severity dot · **FREE** · **STRONG**

### Movement 2 — The Customer (who you'd sell to here)

**4. Who Walks Past Your Door**
Residents vs tourists vs students vs commuters — the four-way footfall body that decides your whole concept. (Dedupes "Who walks past" + "Tourist-vs-local" + the demand-mix half of "Who lives here.")
*Chart:* 100% stacked bar (one bar, four segments) · **FREE** · **MUST-ADD**

**5. Local Spending Power**
How deep local wallets are vs the country and peer cities — the surprising city-vs-country gap. Sizes the wallet, not the crowd. (Dedupes the two spending-power candidates.)
*Chart:* range/dot strip (this city vs country median vs peer band) · **FREE** · **STRONG**

**6. The Demand Calendar**
When the city fills and empties across the year, and who drives each peak — the city's cashflow rhythm, distinct from one trade's seasonality.
*Chart:* line/area sparkline (12-month footfall index, peaks flagged) · **FREE** · **STRONG**

### Movement 3 — The Opportunity (what to open here)

**7. Which Trades Pay Best Here**
The signature cross-trade money menu, ranked, each row routing into its cell. The city page's core navigational spine.
*Chart:* lollipop (trades by owner take-home, dots link down to cells) · **FREE** (top 3 free, rest gated) · **MUST-ADD**

**8. How Crowded Is the Field (Saturation)**
Is your trade already oversupplied here or is there room — the one fear a reader can't Google. Businesses-per-capita vs the typical city.
*Chart:* diverging bar (deviation from all-cities average) · **PREMIUM** · **MUST-ADD**

**9. Can You Hire Here (Talent & Pressure)**
Whether you can staff a business here, at what wage floor, how tight the labour. The staffability read, not raw population. (Dedupes "Can you hire" + "Talent pool/hiring pressure.")
*Chart:* stat-card cluster + small ordered bar of entry-wage vs peers · **PREMIUM** · **STRONG**

### Movement 4 — The Cost (can you afford this city)

**10. Cost to Open Here**
The all-in city-level cash to get a door open — fit-out/sqm, deposit months, licences, working-capital buffer, weeks-to-trade. The single biggest decision gate, owned here. (Dedupes all four "cost to open" candidates.)
*Chart:* funnel (deposit → fit-out → licences → working capital → total) · **PREMIUM** · **MUST-ADD**

**11. The Rent-to-Spending Squeeze**
How much of what locals can spend gets eaten by space cost before you sell anything — the city-level margin tension the score balances. (Dedupes "rent squeeze" + "pay & rent squeeze.")
*Chart:* gauge (rent-burden index, typical-city midpoint marked) · **PREMIUM** · **STRONG**

### Movement 5 — Where In the City (routing to districts)

**12. Where the Money Clusters**
Which districts concentrate footfall and spend before you drill into one — orients the whole set-up decision, hands off to neighbourhoods. (Dedupes "Where the money clusters" + "District picker.")
*Chart:* simple dot map (districts shaded by commercial intensity, dots link to neighbourhoods) · **FREE** · **STRONG**

**13. Rent by Area, Ranked**
Commercial space cost across districts, cheapest to dearest — reframes "can I afford to be central." The full ladder gated.
*Chart:* ordered horizontal bar (districts by £/sqft) · **PREMIUM** · **OPTIONAL**

### Movement 6 — The Comparison & The Bet (city-vs-city + direction)

**14. Peer Cities, On the Metric You Care About**
The like-for-like peer set, comparable on one switchable metric (climate score / rent / spending / cost-to-open). The one allowed cross-geography ranking. (Dedupes all four peer-city candidates + the scoreboard.)
*Chart:* ordered horizontal bar (this city highlighted, metric toggle) · **FREE** headline / **PREMIUM** operational metrics · **MUST-ADD**

**15. Which Way the City Is Moving (Momentum)**
Is the commercial base growing, flat, or shrinking — direction-of-travel turns a snapshot into a 3-year bet. (Dedupes "Which way it's moving" + "Momentum read.")
*Chart:* sparkline trio (formation / footfall / vacancy, each tagged up/flat/down) · **PREMIUM** · **STRONG**

**16. Business Survival Odds**
Once you open here, are you still trading in 3–5 years — the most decision-changing risk fact, near-impossible to find city-by-city. Distinct from one business's exit value (cell).
*Chart:* 100% stacked bar (still trading / closed by cohort year) · **PREMIUM** · **STRONG**

**17. What Locals Know About Trading Here**
The "you find this out after signing the lease" lore — Sunday trading reality, the council parking trap, market-day footfall. High entertainment, genuinely informative, ungoogle-able as a set.
*Chart:* photo cards (4–6 curated local truths, some photo-led) · **PREMIUM** · **STRONG**

**18. The Starter Shortlist**
The synthesis the page builds toward: 3–4 "open this trade → in this district" pairs, each routing to a cell and a neighbourhood. The paid payoff.
*Chart:* photo cards (trade → best district pairs) · **PREMIUM** · **STRONG**

## What I cut, and why

- **Cost-to-Operate Delta vs YOUR base city (operator lens #1)** — cut: a base-city picker with per-line deltas is a complex interactive, and "operate" cost overlaps the squeeze (#11) + cost-to-open (#10). Fold the peer comparison into #14.
- **Cost-to-Operate Index vs peers (investor #1)** — merged into #14 (peer metric toggle) + #11 (squeeze); a separate blended basket double-counts rent/wages already shown.
- **Footfall & Catchment Transport / Demand Depth & Catchment (operator #5, #7)** — cut: a "catchment-within-transit-reach" model is a fabricated-feeling index at city scale and risks restating raw-population-by-another-name. The honest part (movement) lives inside #6 and #12.
- **The Seasonality Mix per peer city (operator #8)** — cut: a per-city demand-source 100% stacked bar collides with #4 and #6 and is precision we can't honestly claim across cities. #4 carries the composition; #6 carries the rhythm.
- **Property Yield Snapshot (investor #3)** — cut: gross commercial yield is a property-investor metric, not a small-business-owner decision; honesty-risky to model rent/capital-value bands per city. Off-persona for the primary reader.
- **Demand Resilience / recession-proofing (investor #7)** — cut: a second modeled composite invites confusion with the Climate Score (only one scored entity allowed) and leans editorial. The honest piece survives as a Red Flag (#3).
- **Small-Business Density as its own bar (investor #4)** — merged into Saturation (#8); same underlying per-capita ratio, one section not two.
- **"Which trades pay best" as an ordered bar AND a lollipop (multiple lenses)** — deduped to one lollipop (#7); the existing take-home table already exists, lollipop adds the at-a-glance spread.
- **Verdict Strip / Best-For tags (operator #12)** — folded into #2 (the honest win/lose decomposition already delivers the screenshot-for-the-partner takeaway).
- **Standalone "Peer cities photo cards" (multiple)** — the discovery/navigation value is real but merged into #14's bar (highlighted + linked); photo-card budget is spent on #17 and #18 where it earns more.
