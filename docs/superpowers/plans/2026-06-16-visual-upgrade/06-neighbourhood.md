# Page type: Neighbourhood overview (micro-market)  (`/[country]/[geo]/[industry]` when it resolves to city + neighbourhood, e.g. `/gb/london/west-end`, `/us/los-angeles/santa-monica`)

## The one job
This page answers one question: "If I open here instead of elsewhere in the city, which trades does this district lift and which does it squeeze, and why?" The single focal point is the answer-first headline that names the district's strongest trade and its one relative number (the top-trade lift vs the city), set on top of a calm gauge. There is deliberately no absolute money at this altitude: a district is a relative read against its own city baseline, nothing else.

## Hero + focal point
The eye lands on ONE thing: the answer-first winner line, e.g. for the West End "Hotels earn about 2x or more versus the rest of London here," with the district name (West End) as the Newsreader H1 above it. The single hero number is the top-trade lift rendered on the multiplier gauge (a `ScoreBand` re-skinned to a 0.4x..3.0x track with the city baseline pinned at 1.0x and a moss-or-atlas marker), NOT an absolute money figure. The primary "answer" is relative-by-design and the page says so. The coordinate links (city name, country flag, "all neighbourhoods") and the character/price/tag pills are demoted: small, quiet, below the band. There is no CTA button at this altitude; the call to action is the clickable `thrives` rows that deepen into the per-trade neighbourhood cells.

## Density & rhythm
Big: the hero (district name + winner line + gauge) and the `thrives` bar set. Those two own the first two screens. Quiet: `who`, `operating-cost`, `ground` are short text-led beats with at most one mini-bar, set in generous whitespace so the page exhales between the two data-heavy moments. The sibling rail closes calm.

The collapse rule: this page type has several genuinely-thin sections on a typical (uncurated) district (street-by-street, sometimes who, sometimes operating-cost, sometimes adjacent, sometimes businesses-here). They do NOT each become a full dashed card. They fold into ONE calm "still filling in" strip, a single stacked group of compact `SectionEmpty` rows (eyebrow + heading + "Not held yet" pill), placed in their locked order but visually unified as one quiet band, so a modeled district reads as a short, intentional page that leads with its strong modeled `thrives` ranking, not as a wall of TODOs. On the filled London exemplar almost nothing collapses; on a bare district only the hero + honest-take + thrives carry weight and the rest is one tidy strip.

## Realness handling
- modeled (`headline`, `thrives`, `operating-cost`, `adjacent`): real computed multipliers from the commuter/tourism/tag engine, but always labelled as modeled and directional. The rail-clamp ("2x or more" / "less than half") is load-bearing honesty: it prevents a wall of identical false-precise "+200%" figures where the multiplier pins to its ceiling. No absolute money is ever printed here.
- london-exemplar (`honest-take`, `one-thing`): the founder-sanctioned filled voice. The honest take is genuinely written from the view (the catchment through-line, the few-streets-over caveat, the rent-eats-the-lift point); it is a brand beat, allowed to be rich because it is editorial, not an invented number.
- real (`who`, prime `streets`, `ground`, `businesses-here`): rendered only from curated `flavor` / `neighborhood_economics` / sibling records. When absent they show a calm `SectionEmpty`: a single muted row reading "[Heading]: Not held yet" with a dot-plus-words tag (non-colour affordance), never a fabricated demographic or street.
- placeholder (street-by-street character): the one section with NO data source anywhere yet. It always reads as the calm `StreetCharacter`/`SectionEmpty` "not mapped yet" state, folded into the collapse strip. Never a fake character tag.

Every placeholder reads as quiet intentional scaffolding (cream ground, dashed parchment hairline, cocoa-700 text clearing AA), never as brokenness and never as a real-looking number.

## Lead-designer QA (page-specific)
The specific risks for THIS page:
1. "+200% everywhere" cringe. The biggest trap. A district like the West End pins many trades to the multiplier ceiling, so naive rendering prints six identical "+200%" rows that scream fabricated. Avoided by the rail-clamp ("2x or more" / "less than half") carried verbatim from the view-model, and by the bars reading as relative magnitude, not false-precise percentages.
2. Mistaking a relative page for an absolute one. Readers expect money. This page has none by design. Avoided by making the gauge explicitly a vs-city-baseline instrument (1.0x pinned, labelled "versus the rest of the city"), and by the honest-take stating it up front, so the absence reads as discipline, not a missing feature.
3. Three kinds of "tag" colliding (character word vs economic tags vs price tier). Avoided by the differentiated chip treatment: price tier as a captioned atlas-tinted pill ("Cost: luxury"), economic tags as quiet cream pills, the character role in the hero break-in chip. Three roles, three visual weights, one accent.
4. The thin-district wall of dashes. Avoided by the single collapse strip rather than per-section dashed cards.
5. Typography drift. Only the district name and the one gauge figure are Newsreader; every label, percent, and body line is Inter with tabular figures and a >=1.25 step. No flat scales, body measure held to 65 to 75ch on the text beats.

The four questions:
- Does it make sense at a glance? Yes. District name, the one winning trade, the one gauge number, in the first screen; everything below answers "why."
- Is it cringe? No, provided the rail-clamp and the relative-by-design framing hold. The danger is only if absolute-money habits or false-precise percentages leak in; both are designed out.
- Does the typography work? Yes. One serif hero number, one accent, tabular figures, generous rhythm, the data blocks (thrives, adjacent) varied against the quiet text beats.
- Can it be made quieter / better? Yes, and it is: the eight-section memo wall is folded into three visual zones, the unheld sections into one strip, the decomposition scoped to one trade so a flat part never over-claims, and absolute money removed entirely at this altitude. That is the quieter, more honest version.
