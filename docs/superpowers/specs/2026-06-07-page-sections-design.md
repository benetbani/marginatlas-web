# Page-Type Sections: The Decided Design (2026-06-07)

The founder-approved target design for all 10 main page types, captured from a 34-question pass (4 on the home page, 30 across the rest). This is the spec to build against. Companion to the research in `2026-06-07-page-sections-brainstorm.md`. Latest taste still applies: clean data tool not magazine, numbers lead, gated-premium-depth this year.

## Cross-cutting rules (the founder's site-wide choices)
- **Empty boxes stay as placeholders** (show the dash). The full data scaffold is kept everywhere even when a fact is missing; it signals what's coming. (Confirmed on both business and country pages.)
- **Numbers lead, almost no prose.** On business pages: NO written paragraphs, numbers only. Site-wide, written explanation is minimal.
- **No verdict sentence at the top.** Let the numbers lead; do not open pages with a one-line "Yes, but..." verdict.
- **Name-the-catch tone only where it's real.** Pair an upside with its killer when there's a genuine risk, never forced.
- **Mobile shows everything, stacked.** No trimming on small screens; stack vertically.
- **Link onward everywhere.** Make the site one connected web; every page pushes to related pages (same business elsewhere, other businesses here, cost-to-open, etc.).
- **Headline 0-100 score: business pages AND city pages only.** Countries and industries stay score-free (number-rich, no headline score).
- Estimates carry a subtle "modeled" label (one quiet line per block); when unsure show one tagged estimate, not a dash or range (from the prior vision pass).

## 1. Home (`/`) — DONE/shipped
- **Role: a launchpad to search.** Lead with the search box (as now).
- Fake "neighborhood proof" panel REPLACED with live district data (shipped). Editorial TRIMMED to essentials, one data beat (shipped).
- Flow: search hero, world map, eight-forces thesis, break-in rankings, live cities panel, one leaderboard beat, operator questions, how-we-know, blog.

## 2. Business-in-a-place page (`/[country]/[geo]/[industry]`) — the core
- **Lead under the score: what you'd earn** (revenue + owner take-home first).
- **Empty sections: keep, shown with dashes** (full scaffold).
- **Bottom tail: reorder by usefulness** (keep the failure cards / nearby businesses / related types, but order most-useful first, weakest last).
- **Cut the prose.** Remove the narrative paragraph, the local-context note, and the "if you opened today" timeline. Numbers only. (Reversible, but the founder chose numbers-only.)
- Score badge + masthead unchanged; the "what it takes to open" section + the deep board stay.
- Heavy onward links (this business elsewhere, other businesses here, cost-to-open).

## 3. Cost-to-open page (`/[country]/[geo]/[industry]/opening`)
- **Stays upfront-only.** Capital, time, permits, first hires. Do NOT add ongoing/monthly running costs or break-even here (the business page covers that).
- **Add a "don't open unless..." line ONLY where there's a genuine dealbreaker** (not on every page).
- Keep it the tight, disciplined 4-part page it is.

## 4. Country page (`/[country]`)
- **Lead: the business climate** (taxes, wages, how easy to set up).
- **Consolidate the three tax/registration spots into ONE strong "what the government takes and how hard to set up" section.** Cut the other two.
- **Empty sections (fragility, friction): keep as placeholders** (dashes).
- No headline score (countries stay score-free).

## 5. City / region pages
- **Region pages become simple indexes** that point to their cities. Cities carry the depth.
- **On region pages, replace the (wrong) country-level top-industries list with the region's CITIES.**
- **City pages lead with the demand picture** (people, income, tourists, who the customers are).
- **Cities get a headline 0-100 score** (the only place-type that does).
- Cities are the rich place page; unify the two place routes so the real city board powers the city experience.

## 6. Cities directory (`/cities`)
- **Group cities by region of the world** (Europe, Asia, Americas, ...).
- **Add a predictive city-name search bar** (type-ahead autocomplete over the covered city names). [Founder write-in.]
- Keep the map; the region grouping + search replace the need for heavy rankings.

## 7. Industry page (`/industries/[industry]`)
- **Lead with the "pick a place" tool** (action first, get them to a place fast).
- **Merge the two place-pickers** (the ranked list and the country buttons) into ONE clear picker.
- The business-model anatomy / where-the-dollar-goes stays below the picker.

## 8. Comparison page (`/industries/[industry]/across`, `/compare`) — the page to PERFECT
- **The founder's #1 page to get exactly right.** Treat as the craft priority alongside business pages.
- **Merge the two "best place to break in" call-outs into one.**
- **No "why A beats B" sentence** , the side-by-side table makes the reason clear.
- Keep the side-by-side dashboard with honest spreads + the break-in row + cost-to-open links.

## 9. Extremes hub (`/extremes`)
- **Lead with cheapest/priciest to open** (cost-to-open rankings lead, not take-home).
- **Make it filterable** , let people choose the angle (take-home, break-in, crowding, cost, and more) rather than a fixed stack.

## 10. Tools: Decide / Check / Calculator
- **Fold all three into ONE tools hub** so they don't compete.
- **Check ("compare your numbers") expands to every business** we cover (from the current 20).
- **Decide's hardcoded example cards become real, strong live picks.**

## Roadmap from this pass
- **Next NEW page type to build: "Buy vs start"** , for a business-and-place, is it smarter to buy an existing one or start fresh? An acquisition angle that fits the investor/buyer audience.
- **Page to perfect first: the comparison page** (the founder's pick). Make the side-by-side the sharpest decision surface on the site.

## Suggested build order (concrete, lowest-risk first)
1. Quick honesty/clarity wins: consolidate the country tax sections; region pages → point to cities (drop the wrong industry list); merge the industry place-pickers; cut the business-page prose.
2. The comparison page polish (the priority-to-perfect): merge the call-outs, tighten the side-by-side.
3. City headline score + unify the city route; extremes lead/filter; cities-directory region grouping + search bar.
4. Tools hub + Check expansion + Decide live examples.
5. New "Buy vs start" page type.
Every data/render change dry-run + screenshot-verified + shipped per the standing discipline.
