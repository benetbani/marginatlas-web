# Atlas Brand Identity (2026-06-12)

The brand, from 40 founder decisions (round 4 of the direction quiz). This governs everything
visual and emotional. Lives in `docs/brand/` (the single brand home). Feeds the Design System
constitution (next) and every page.

---

## Essence
- **Archetype:** a wise, generous guide. Knows the terrain better than anyone, shares it openly to help you.
- **What it is, in one line:** the world's honest map of what businesses really make.
- **Emotional promise:** clarity you can trust, on a decision that scares you.
- **Why it exists:** because nobody tells you the real numbers before you risk everything.
- **The one word to own:** TRUSTWORTHY (honest). Trust is the moat; everything sits on it.

## The feeling (psychology)
- On arrival: calm, confident clarity. (Not hype, not overwhelm, not cold.)
- About your decision: clear-eyed and empowered, never pressured.
- Temperature: warm, but serious about money.
- The relationship: a trusted friend who happens to be the world expert.
- Positioning: timeless authority with a challenger's honesty. Global scope, local soul.
- NEVER: a cold database, a hype startup, or a corporate consultancy.

## The visual world
- A beautiful modern atlas / field almanac. Cartographic heritage, modern execution.
- Light, warm, paper-like (cream/parchment), not stark white or dark.
- A whisper of paper/engraving texture on a fundamentally clean surface. Heritage soul, never a costume.
- Vibe in a phrase: editorial magazine with cartographic heritage.

## Color
- Signature palette KEPT + refined: deep vermillion-maroon (#991600) + bright vermillion (#e62200) accent, on warm cream; ink near-black text; cocoa; one quiet moss green as the only secondary.
- Restrained: ink + cream canvas, vermillion as the rare highlight.
- Color's job: a calm canvas where accents MARK MEANING (the key number, the honest take, a warning), never decorate.

## Typography
- Display: a distinctive editorial SERIF (warm, confident, timeless, the almanac/magazine soul).
- Body: a clean humanist SANS (readable for long text + dense numbers).
- A SIGNATURE display face, recognizable as Atlas, worth investing in.
- **OPEN ACTION:** the founder is unsure of the current display font. Re-evaluate and likely upgrade. NEXT: build a font-showcase preview page with 3-4 hand-picked serif candidates on a real Atlas page so the founder can FEEL and choose, not guess.

## Imagery (a core part of the brand)
- Rich and immersive, not decoration. Beautiful imagery is part of what makes Atlas elegant and alive.
- Documentary style: real places, real crafts, dignified, never stock-y.
- Mostly real PHOTOGRAPHY + editorial ILLUSTRATION for abstract ideas (the honest take, vs-the-world).
- Natural, warm color, lightly toned toward the brand. Cohesive but honest, never a heavy filter.
- Subject: places and the people / crafts in them. The dignity of real work.
- Respectful realism: every place + trade shown as it truly is, with equal care (a Tokyo sushi counter and a Lagos market stall). Never exotic spectacle, never stereotype.
- Local authenticity within a consistent frame: each place looks like itself; a shared treatment unifies.
- **Sourcing (ethical):** real, curated/commissioned photography wherever possible; labeled if not; NEVER pass off a generic or AI image as a specific real place. Honesty extends to the imagery.

## Craft, elegance, dignity
- Elegant, but never elitist: premium craft in service of everyday people.
- Genuine respect for every subject, no condescension toward a "humble" trade or a "poor" country.
- Heritage soul, modern hands: established and trustworthy, yet effortless and current.
- Openly celebrates the crafts and places it covers, with reverence. Made by people who care.

## Identity devices
- Wordmark: the name in the signature serif + a small, elegant cartographic mark (a coordinate, a contour line). Not a generic tech logo.
- Icons: fine line, one consistent family (the icon bundle from the design prompts).
- Signature through-line device: a subtle CARTOGRAPHIC MOTIF (fine contour rules, coordinate labels, engraved edges) running quietly across every page, so any Atlas page is recognizable at a glance.
- Motion: quiet and precise, like turning a page. Never bouncy or flashy.

---

## In one breath
Atlas is the world's honest map of what businesses really make: a wise, generous guide that
makes you feel calm, clear-eyed, and capable on a decision that scares you. It looks like a
beautiful modern almanac (warm cream, ink, a rare stroke of vermillion, a distinctive serif, a
quiet cartographic thread, rich and dignified real imagery), it feels like a trusted friend who
is also the world expert, and above all it earns one word: trustworthy.
