# Margin Atlas — Long-Term Vision + Roadmap

**Date:** 2026-06-06. Derived from a 20-question founder taste-and-vision discovery. This is the north star; it sits alongside REFORMATION-BIBLE.md (product spec) and guides every future content/feature decision.

## North star

**"The reference atlas of business profitability."** A warm, premium, magazine-rich authority that buyers, investors, and would-be operators browse to answer one question: **where can I break in and actually make money?** Broad as the world, deep enough to trust, beautiful enough to read for pleasure, and citeable enough that professionals build on it through the API.

## The vision, in the founder's own choices

1. **Audience first:** buyers and investors judging "is this market worth entering or buying," served warmly enough that a nervous first-timer also belongs.
2. **Coverage:** broad. Every business in every place, at programmatic scale. The map is the moat.
3. **Voice:** warm and encouraging, a mentor. An evolution AWAY from the current blunt/contrarian register (keep the honesty, lose the coldness).
4. **Look:** magazine-rich, FULL editorial. Photography, written stories, a true publication feel. A deliberate evolution AWAY from stark white minimalism.
5. **The decisive question:** ease of entry + room to win. The market-attractiveness score leans hardest on ease of entry (setup cost, friction, barriers); competition density is the next dataset to add.
6. **New surfaces:** comparison pages (one business across places), cost-to-open pages (the full checklist), a market-attractiveness score, and a dedicated "extremes" leaderboard hub.
7. **Monetization:** free site + paid API. The data is the product; pages stay lean and static; pros pay for programmatic access.
8. **Growth engine:** programmatic pages at scale. SEO flywheel across every business-and-place and every new page type.
9. **Identity:** the reference atlas. Cited, browsable, premium. Not a Bloomberg terminal, not a Zillow; its own warm, authoritative category.

## The productive tension (the most important insight)

The product today is a stark-white, blunt, lean data tool. The founder's taste is warm, magazine-rich, full-editorial, premium-reference. The long-term arc is to resolve that tension by ADDITION, not replacement: keep the dashboard-first rigor, the honest dashes, the decision-first order and the no-fake-numbers bar; then wrap them in warmth (mentor voice), imagery (tasteful photography), and narrative (real stories) so the site reads as a beloved reference book. The cold quant surface becomes the API.

## The next paths (content, sections, pages) shaped by the vision

### New page types (the chosen growth lever)
- **Comparison pages** — "[business] across places" (e.g. restaurants: Miami vs Barcelona vs London), generated programmatically, side by side, built for fast buyer judgment. The chosen default comparison.
- **Cost-to-open pages** — the full checklist per business-and-place: capital to start, time to open, permits and licensing, first hires. The entry number buyers need.
- **Market-attractiveness score** — a single ease-of-entry-weighted read on every cell ("can I get in and win here"), and a ranking dimension.
- **The Extremes hub** — a dedicated, browsable leaderboard of the surprising highs and lows across the whole dataset. The addictive front-of-house.

### Enrich what exists
- **Competition density** as a real dataset (firms per capita, saturation) — powers the score and the "room to enter" read. The chosen next dataset.
- **Editorial layer** — written stories + tasteful photography on flagship pages; the magazine direction. Country/city/business mastheads carry atmosphere (we already restored country images).
- **Warm-voice pass** — rewrite the microcopy registers from blunt toward mentor-encouraging, without losing the honesty (still name the killer, but kindly).

### The data product
- **The API** — structured, programmatic access for professionals. Define the free/paid line: free site for reach and citation, paid API for the people who build on the data.

## Roadmap (phased)

- **This quarter (the chosen priority, "the new pages AND the magazine redesign" together):**
  - Ship the comparison pages, the cost-to-open pages, and the market-attractiveness score.
  - Inside a magazine-rich, warm-voiced redesign: editorial imagery + a story layer on flagship pages, and the microcopy warmed throughout.
- **Next:** competition-density dataset → score + room-to-enter; launch the Extremes hub.
- **Then:** the API + paid data layer; scale the programmatic generation across all business-and-place combinations and the new page types.
- **Foundation, ongoing:** the data-quality work already scoped (tighten loose bounds, currency beyond Mexico, the remap + band-dedupe) — the trust the warm reference rests on. A premium reference cannot show a wrong number.

## How this changes day-to-day decisions
- Every new section: does it help a buyer judge "can I break in and win here," and does it read warm and premium, not cold?
- Every page: dashboard-first data, then a story, then atmosphere. Honest dashes stay.
- Every number: still no fake figures. The warmth is in the voice and the design, never in inflating the data.
