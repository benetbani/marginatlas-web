The masthead scorecard, before and after

Both halves are the real masthead, server rendered. Every word and figure is byte for byte identical, checked node by node.

Narrow this window to phone width and read the three tiles under the big number. In the lower one, "Demanding" prints as "Deman" and the count of firms already trading here is cut off at its right edge. Three fixed columns with no width rule at all, inside a box that hides its overflow: on a phone each tile got about fifty pixels of room for a figure set at twenty, and the box quietly swallowed the evidence. Two of the three readings in the page's own scorecard were unreadable on the width most people use.

They stack now, and go back to three across once there is room. The library's own stats grid does exactly that; the block itself is a section-wide band with its own heading and does not belong inside a masthead, so the convention was taken and the block declined.
