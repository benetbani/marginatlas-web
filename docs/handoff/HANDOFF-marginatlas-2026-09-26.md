## 4. How we got here, the decision trail

- **A study before a line of code.** PLAN.md section 2 names what a reader's mind does with a card (overview first, chunks of
  about four, recognition over recall, the self-reference effect, answer then why, proximity, affordance and feedback, access),
  section 3 turns them into seven mechanisms with a law and a gate each, section 4 audits every card of the UK page and the trade
  sections against them. Every change since cites a row of that table.
- **One wrapper, not one hover per chart.** `Marks` (interact/Marks.tsx) reads `data-readout-figure`, `data-readout-words` and
  `data-part-key` off any drawing's marks, so eight drawings learned to answer without eight implementations, and the gate reads
  one contract. The attribute names were renamed once because `data-marks` and `data-part` already meant something else.
- **A lever only where the card holds the rule**, and its default is the figure the card prints: the hire at the average
  salary, all covers ticked, the country's curve, the largest loan over the longest term. Never a made-up "typical" input.
- **The sense fixes came first** (batch 41's B): the insurance figure was a legal minimum of cover that read as a $6.6M cost; the
  time to sell was a count of countries to be inverted; legal and admin mixed yearly costs with closing ones; the spending bar's
  figure was worked from two parts nothing marked; the margins' figure was the middle trade with no middle drawn.
- **Tables: the sort's first press follows the tick** (best first), the home row keeps its tint, the opening order is the
  builder's; the legal forms' chevron became his plus (his 2026-09-08 vocabulary for "click and more appears").
- **Icons: a cell never repeats its card's icon, a glyph the set draws is reused, a new glyph is judged at 18px beside the set.**
- **Blank space is measured, not guessed**: the icons grew each label line by 10px and tipped two cards over the page filter's
  floor; a lone cell over a pair cannot absorb a lent height without a blank beside it or at its foot, so three cells go three
  across in a wide grid and the legal year's facts stack (scratchpad/b44_measure.mjs printed the rows).

## 5. Hard-won truths & mental model

- **A block comment as a JSX text child prints on the page.** `<div>` then a line `/* ... */` then `<Marks>` renders the comment
  as text. `no-internal-notes` caught two before they shipped; the comment goes in braces or above the markup.
- **The census finds a card by its own `data-archetype` stamp.** When a section hands its drawing to an interact control (the
  survival curve), the section stops being an archetype to the census; census.ts now counts a component that renders a Box and
  draws an archetype of the four folders.
- **The copy gate counts `<p>` lines at the micro rung.** A lever's second sentence ("$40K repaid over 5 years") is a second
  supporting line; a total drawn as a figure with a two-word unit is not.
- **The model laws' ladder forbids 17 to 29px in a card that holds a 30**: a lever's answer is the lead rung, 16.
- **WHITE SPACE and CARD FOOT BLANK pull against each other** in a stretched card: a blank at least max(120, a quarter of the card)
  wide and tall is a fault, and so is more than 48px under the last ink. Spread spare height over three or more rows, or fill it.
- **Playwright hovers a donut wedge at the circle's centre**, which is not on the stroke: drive a wedge at its arc's midpoint
  (`getPointAtLength` and `getScreenCTM`), as `scratchpad/b42_live_interact.mjs` does.
- **Flags lazy-load**: a card photographed without scrolling into view drops every flag but the hero's.
- **Each Marks drawing owns its own panel**: `document.querySelector("[data-readout-panel]")` returns the first on the page, not
  the open one; read the panel inside the drawing.
- **Windows memory**: browser checks stop under 620MB free, the chain waits for 760MB; his apps hold the rest and are never
  closed. Wait with an until-loop, then run.

## 6. Dead ends, do not retry

- A lone key-value row at its natural height with the full rows sharing the spare (`grid-template-rows: auto 1fr`): it moved the
  air to the card's foot (62px, clause 52).
- Three key-value cells three across inside a stretched card: one row cannot hold the spare without a foot or top blank.
- A clock glyph of our own: the set's `freshness` already draws it.
- The shop-shutter glyph for closing: an archive box at 18px; the padlock reads.
- A plain plus in a rounded square for sick pay: reads as "add"; the first-aid kit reads.
- Checking a readout by the first `[data-readout-panel]` in the document (see section 5).

## 11. Landmines & gotchas

- Scratch copies of components (`*.full.tsx`) under `website/scratchpad/` break a local `tsc` (the tsconfig includes every tsx);
  keep them out of the site tree.
- `sed -i` on a CRLF file keeps its endings; Python patches must read with `newline=""` and write the file's own ending back.
- A heredoc holding Python with backslashes or nested quotes fails in bash; write the script with the Write tool.
