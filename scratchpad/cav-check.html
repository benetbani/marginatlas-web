+ The claims, in full
  • The 90% first-year failure claim is a myth; roughly four in five survive year one.
  • High gross margin is misleading; labor and rent consume most of it, leaving low single-digit net.
  • Full tables do not guarantee profit; prime cost creeping above 65% wipes out the margin.

Survival is better than folklore suggests, but the margin is so thin that food and labor cost control, not popularity, decides who makes money.
