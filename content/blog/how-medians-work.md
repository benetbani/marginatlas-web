Imagine 11 small bakeries. Ten earn between $200k and $800k. The 11th, a chain spinoff, earns $50M.

- Average: $5M ← misleading
- Median: $500k ← honest
- Smallest 10% (p10): $200k
- Biggest 10% (p90): $800k (ignoring the outlier)
