Different countries use different classification systems for industries. The major ones:

- **ISIC Rev.4**: UN standard, 4-digit, used internationally as a reference
- **NAICS 2017**: North American standard (US/CA/MX), 6-digit
- **NACE Rev.2**: European standard, 4-digit, identical to ISIC at 2-digit level
- **SSIC**: Singapore, 5-digit
- **PKD 2007**: Poland, follows NACE
- **WZ2008**: Germany, follows NACE
- **ANZSIC**: Australia/New Zealand, 4-digit

The good news: at the section level (A-U), they're all roughly aligned. The bad news: at the 4-6 digit level, boundaries diverge.
