Our policy on extrapolation:

**We extrapolate when**:
- The country has reliable World Bank macro data (GDP, employment, urbanization)
- We have well-covered comparator countries (US, EU, AU, CA) to anchor on
- We can show statistical confidence intervals

**We stay silent when**:
- The country lacks even macro data (e.g., Eritrea, North Korea)
- Comparator countries are too different (e.g., extrapolating to tiny island states)
- Data privacy would be violated (cells with <3 firms)

Every extrapolated cell is tagged `coverage_tier='X'` and shown with a clear 'Estimated from regional patterns' badge. We never disguise extrapolation as direct data.
