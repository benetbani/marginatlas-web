Italy's economy is uniquely family-firm-dominated:

- 4.2M businesses
- ~85% family-owned (vs 30% in US, 60% in Germany)
- Median firm: 3 employees
- Highly fragmented in tourism, retail, manufacturing
- 'Industrial districts' (Prato textiles, Como silk, etc.) drive specialty exports
