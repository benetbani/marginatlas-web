Argentine SMBs operate under unique conditions:

- 1.4M registered businesses
- High inflation (>100% YoY 2022-2024) reshapes business decisions
- USD-pegged contracts common
- Median firm: 6 employees
- Buenos Aires + Córdoba + Rosario = 55% of businesses
