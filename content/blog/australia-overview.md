Australian SMB profile:

- 2.4M businesses
- 97% are micro/small
- Top sectors: professional services, retail, construction
- 65% of businesses on the east coast (NSW + VIC + QLD)
- High self-employment rate (~18% of workforce)
- ABS publishes full state and SA2 (smaller-area) granularity
