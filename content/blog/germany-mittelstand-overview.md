Germany has ~3.7M businesses. Unique to Germany is the Mittelstand: family-owned, specialized, mid-sized firms (10-499 employees) that dominate global exports in their niches.

- 99.4% of German businesses are SMEs
- 60% of employment
- 35% of total revenue
- Manufacturing leads: 22% of all employees vs ~9% in US
