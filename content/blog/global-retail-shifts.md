E-commerce share of retail varies enormously:
- USA: ~21% of retail sales online
- China: ~38%
- UK: ~28%
- Germany: ~19%
- France: ~17%
- Italy: ~12%
- Japan: ~14%

The number of physical retail firms has declined 12-25% in most developed economies since 2018, while the number of e-commerce firms has 2-4x'd.
