FIPS codes (Federal Information Processing Standards) identify US states with 2-digit numbers:

- 06 California, 12 Florida, 36 New York, 48 Texas
- ... 56 codes total (50 states + DC + territories)

In our URLs we use slugs (`/us/california`) for friendliness. Internally each state has its FIPS code. Browse `/us` to see all.
