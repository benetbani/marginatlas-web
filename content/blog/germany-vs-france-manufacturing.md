title: "Manufacturing in Germany vs France: the productivity gap"
date: "2026-05-12"
excerpt: "Both countries have strong manufacturing bases, but German manufacturers are noticeably bigger and more productive per worker."
author: "Margin Atlas team"
---

German manufacturing (NACE C) and French manufacturing (NACE C) tell two different stories.

In Germany, the median manufacturer employs **18 people** and brings in **€2.8M** in yearly revenue — about **€155k revenue per employee**.

In France, the median manufacturer employs **9 people** and brings in **€1.1M** in revenue — about **€122k per employee**.

Why the gap?

1. **Scale**: German firms have had decades of consolidation; French manufacturers stay smaller longer
2. **Specialization**: the German Mittelstand model rewards focused mid-sized firms
3. **Productivity capital investment**: German firms invest more in machinery per worker

The gap is real but narrowing. France has caught up significantly in software-adjacent manufacturing (med devices, robotics) since 2018.
