Finance and insurance (NACE K, NAICS 52) is concentrated:

- USA, UK, Switzerland, Singapore, Hong Kong, Luxembourg = ~70% of global activity
- Per-firm revenue varies wildly: $400k median in Greece vs $12M+ in Switzerland

What's striking is the *sub-sector mix*. Switzerland is wealth management heavy. Singapore is banking-services heavy. London is trading-floor heavy. New York spans all three.
