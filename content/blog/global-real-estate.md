US real estate agencies are 3-4x larger per-firm than European equivalents. Why?

- US: each agent typically belongs to a firm but operates as 1099 contractor under it. Firms are aggregators of independent agents.
- Europe: agents are usually full employees of estate agencies. Firms are smaller, more bounded.

This means 'number of firms' and 'firms per capita' aren't directly comparable. Margin Atlas presents both raw counts AND adjustments where possible.
