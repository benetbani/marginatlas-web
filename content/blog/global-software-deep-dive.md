Software is unevenly distributed across the world in ways that other industries aren't.

Global cells we have:
- USA: 78,000 software firms, median 6 employees, median $1.4M revenue
- UK: 35,000 firms
- Germany: 32,000
- France: 28,000
- Sweden: 14,000 (extraordinary per-capita)
- India: 22,000 formal (millions more informal)
- Israel: 3,800 (highest per-capita anywhere)
- Singapore: 4,200

The global software sector is now larger than US auto manufacturing in employment terms.
