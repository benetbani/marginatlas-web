Every cell page on Margin Atlas has six sections. Here's what each one tells you.

### 1. Hero
A plain-English statement of the typical number. Often the only thing a casual visitor reads.

### 2. Four stats
Firm count, employee count, typical revenue, typical wage. These are the most-cited numbers in any benchmark.

### 3. The distribution bars
Five horizontal bars showing the spread from smallest 10% to biggest 10%. This is the *real* shape of the industry, not just the average.

### 4. Data quality badge
Five-star rating + source line. Five stars = direct primary survey. Two stars = modeled estimate. Always check this.

### 5. Comparable cells
Other industries in the same place. Useful for sanity-checking and exploring nearby businesses.

### 6. Methodology link
For the curious: every number's provenance.
