Manufacturing is in long-term relative decline as a share of GDP, but in absolute terms it's still massive. Our data shows:

- Total cells: ~28,000 manufacturing × country × size band combinations
- Median manufacturing SMB: 22 employees, $3.2M revenue
- Most concentrated in: China (informally), Germany, Italy, Japan
- Highest per-firm revenue: Switzerland, Singapore (premium niches)

Three archetypes:
1. **German Mittelstand**: specialized mid-size firms, family-owned, export-heavy
2. **Italian district**: small clustered firms in specialty regions
3. **US scale**: fewer, much larger firms
