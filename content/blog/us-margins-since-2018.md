Run the same NAICS-6 cell across 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2022 SUSB releases. What stands out?

1. **The median is shockingly stable.** Across most industries, median per-firm revenue moved 8-15% nominally, basically tracking inflation.
2. **The top decile widened.** Top-10% revenue grew faster than median in almost every industry, especially software, e-commerce, and warehousing.
3. **The bottom decile recovered.** Solo proprietors saw a sharp 2020 drop, then a strong 2021-2022 recovery.

What this suggests: COVID created a brief shock, but the long-term story is just gradual inflation plus the steady winning of bigger firms. Nothing structural broke.
