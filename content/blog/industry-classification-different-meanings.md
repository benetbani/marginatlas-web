Classification standards (NAICS, NACE, ISIC, SSIC, ANZSIC) exist precisely so industry data is comparable. But the devil is in the boundary cases.

Example: a sushi takeaway counter inside a Japanese supermarket.

- In the US (NAICS): likely classified as 7225 Restaurants (the dominant activity at the counter)
- In the EU (NACE): often classified as 47.11 Retail sale in non-specialised stores (food predominant), because it's inside a grocery store
- In Japan (e-Stat): classified separately under takeaway food preparation

For cross-country comparison this means a small but real over/undercount in some industries.
