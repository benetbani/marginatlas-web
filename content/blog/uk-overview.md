UK SMB statistics from ONS:

- 5.5M total businesses
- 76% are sole proprietorships
- Median small employer firm: 6 employees, £490k revenue
- Top sectors: professional services, retail, construction
- Post-Brexit: increased SMB exports to non-EU markets
