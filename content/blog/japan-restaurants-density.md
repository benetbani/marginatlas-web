Tokyo has ~140,000 restaurants for ~14 million residents — that's 1 restaurant per 100 people.

New York has ~25,000 for ~8 million — 1 per 320 people.

London: 1 per 250.
Paris: 1 per 180.
Milan: 1 per 200.

What enables this?

1. **Tiny formats**: Japanese restaurants average 12 seats vs ~75 in US/UK
2. **Owner-operator model**: 78% of Tokyo restaurants are owner-staffed
3. **Pre-prep automation**: standardized supply chains keep margins viable at small scale
4. **Late-night culture + commuter flow**: same physical seat serves 4 day-parts

Margins are tight (typical EBITDA ~8-10%) but volume keeps the businesses alive.
