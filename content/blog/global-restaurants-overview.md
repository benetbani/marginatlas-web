Restaurant businesses (NACE 56, NAICS 722) make up 4-8% of small businesses in every developed country.

Key global patterns:

- **Median revenue per firm**: $250k–$800k
- **Median employees per firm**: 4–12
- **EBITDA margin**: typically 6-12% (thin, but stable)
- **Failure rate**: 15-25% in the first 5 years

Geographic variance:
- Highest per-firm revenue: California, Switzerland, Singapore (~$1M+)
- Lowest: rural Eastern Europe (€80-180k)
- Best operating margins: Japan, Switzerland, Germany (8-12%)
- Highest survival rate: Japan (87% at 5 years)
