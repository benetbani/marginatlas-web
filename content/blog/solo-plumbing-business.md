title: "What it takes to run a single-employee plumbing business"
date: "2026-05-10"
excerpt: "The single-person plumbing firm is one of the most common business shapes in the developed world. The numbers say it works, but barely."
author: "Margin Atlas team"
---

Across 30 countries, single-employee plumbing businesses (NAICS 238220 in the US, NACE 43.22 in the EU) make up the largest chunk of the trade.

The typical solo plumber's firm in the US earns about **$135,000 in yearly revenue**. After materials (~$30k), insurance (~$8k), tools and truck (~$15k), and taxes, the owner takes home around $55,000–$70,000.

That's a job, not a business. And it's the most common shape in the trade.

The transition from solo to small (2-9 employees) is where the wealth shows up: those firms earn $480k–$700k typically, and the owner's take is 2-3× the solo plumber's.
