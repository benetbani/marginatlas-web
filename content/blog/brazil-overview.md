Brazil's SMB landscape:

- ~12M formal businesses (IBGE CEMPRE)
- ~10M+ informal microenterprises (estimated)
- Median formal firm: 4 employees
- Geographic concentration: São Paulo + Rio de Janeiro = 35% of all firms
- Top sectors: retail, services, construction
