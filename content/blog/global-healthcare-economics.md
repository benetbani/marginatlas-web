Healthcare (NACE Q, NAICS 62) has unique economics:
- Doctors offices: highly profitable, regulated supply
- Dental practices: high capital, high cash flow
- Physiotherapy/clinics: low capital, labor-intensive
- Nursing/elderly care: low margin, government-influenced

US has the highest per-firm revenue ($1.8M typical doctor's office) due to insurance billing dynamics. European systems show lower per-firm revenue but higher firm counts per capita.
