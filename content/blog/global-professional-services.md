Professional services SMBs:
- Highly educated workforce (avg degree-or-higher >85% of employees)
- High per-employee revenue ($200-400k)
- Wide variance: legal services typically 2x consulting revenue

The US has 1.4M professional services firms (vs ~1M in all EU combined). Per capita: US has 4.3 firms per 1,000 inhabitants vs EU average 2.2.
