Solo proprietors — single-person businesses with no employees — are the most underreported workforce in economics.

In the United States there are roughly **27 million** of them. In France, ~5 million. In Germany, ~4 million. As a share of the workforce, the range is **8% (Germany) to 18% (Italy)** to **22% (Greece)**.

Margin Atlas captures them as the '1' employee size band on every industry page. The data is real and primary-sourced — but it's also the noisiest tier, because reporting requirements vary.

When you see 'Solo (1 person)' on a cell page, what you're seeing is the working economy of independent practitioners, lifestyle businesses, and side hustles that don't fit any other category.
