US Census distinguishes:

- **Employer firms** (~6M): pay at least one W-2 employee. Subject to SUSB.
- **Nonemployer firms** (~27M): solo proprietorships, sole prop S-corps, no W-2 employees. Subject to NES.

Nonemployers represent 80% of US businesses by count but only 4% of total revenue. They're the gig economy, freelancers, and side-hustles.
