France's small business landscape was transformed by the 2009 micro-entrepreneur status. Since then:

- Total registered legal units: 29.5M
- Of which truly active (status=A): ~12M
- Median PME: 9 employees, €890k revenue
- Top sectors: real estate, professional services, retail
