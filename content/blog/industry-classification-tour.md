Major industry classification standards:

- **ISIC Rev.4** (UN) — universal reference
- **NAICS 2017** (US/CA/MX)
- **NACE Rev.2** (EU)
- **SSIC 2020** (Singapore)
- **JSIC** (Japan)
- **PKD 2007** (Poland)
- **ANZSIC 2006** (Australia/NZ)
- **CNAE 2.0** (Brazil)

At the **section** level (A-U) they're nearly identical. At **2-digit** they're 80%+ aligned. At **4-6 digit** they diverge significantly.

We build crosswalks at the 2-digit level (most useful for cross-country comparison) and preserve native codes for users who need exactness.
