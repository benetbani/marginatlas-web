On paper Australia and Canada look like economic twins: similar population (~26 vs ~40 million), commodity-heavy exports, English-speaking, federation structure.

In small-business detail they're surprisingly different.

**Australian small businesses** are more **service-oriented** and **smaller per-firm**: median Australian business has 4 employees and earns A$680k.

**Canadian small businesses** are more **manufacturing-tilted** and **larger per-firm**: median Canadian SMB has 7 employees and C$1.1M revenue.

Why? Canada's manufacturing base (auto parts, aerospace) skews its average up. Australia's gold/iron mining is dominated by giants, but the SMB tail is heavy in services + hospitality.
