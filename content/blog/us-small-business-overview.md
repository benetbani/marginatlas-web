The US has the most comprehensive small-business statistics in the world.

- ~33M businesses total
- ~27M solo proprietorships
- ~6M employer firms
- Largest size class: 1-9 employees (78% of employer firms)
- Median revenue: ~$420k across all industries
- Median employees per firm: 4

The US Census Bureau publishes SUSB, CBP, ZBP at state, county, and ZIP level. We've ingested all of them. Browse any US state's industry breakdown at /us.
