Spain's autónomo system creates a unique structure:

- 3.4M employer businesses
- 3.3M autónomos (~self-employed independent contractors)
- Combined: ~14% of the working-age population
- Sectors: services dominate (tourism, hospitality, professional services)
- Mediterranean coastal tourism employs ~12% of total workforce
