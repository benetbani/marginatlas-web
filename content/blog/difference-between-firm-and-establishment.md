---
title: "A firm vs an establishment — why the difference matters"
date: "2026-05-09"
excerpt: "Both terms appear in our data. They mean different things."
author: "Margin Atlas team"
---

Common confusion:

- **Firm/enterprise**: a legal entity. Walmart Inc. is one firm.
- **Establishment**: a physical location. Walmart has ~4,700 establishments.

Our cells show **firms** for the headline 'how many firms' number, and **employees per firm**.

For multi-location chains, our data captures one entry per firm (with total revenue + employees across all locations).

Sole proprietorships always = 1 firm = 1 establishment.
