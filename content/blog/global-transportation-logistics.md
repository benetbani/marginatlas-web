author: "Margin Atlas team"
---

Global pattern:
- ~5-7% of small businesses are in transport/logistics
- ~70% of those are 1-person operations (owner-operator truckers, taxi drivers)
- Median revenue per firm: $120k-$280k
- Margins thin (4-9% typical)

The rise of ride-share has eaten dramatically into single-cab taxi numbers since 2018:
- US: -38% taxi-only firms
- UK: -42%
- France: -35%

But total passenger-transport activity is UP — rides are just spread across more (smaller) operators.
