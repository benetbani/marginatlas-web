Construction is universally important. Across our 40+ countries, construction firms represent 7-12% of all businesses and 6-9% of total employment.

The pattern is remarkably consistent:

- **Many tiny firms**: typically 60-70% are 1-9 person operations
- **Specialty trades dominate**: plumbing, electrical, roofing — these alone are 40-50% of construction firms
- **Low capital, high labor**: median revenue per employee is consistently $80-130k
- **Local market lock-in**: construction businesses rarely scale beyond a metropolitan area

Where countries diverge:
- US: heavy commercial mid-market
- Germany: large general contractors lead
- Italy: family-owned small operators dominate
- China: state-owned giants + millions of informal workers
