Poland's construction sector has been one of Europe's quiet success stories.

GUS data shows:
- 2018: 410,000 construction firms, mean revenue PLN 1.8M (~$420k)
- 2022: 480,000 construction firms, mean revenue PLN 2.6M (~$610k)

+45% revenue growth nominally, +28% real. Faster than nearly any other Polish sector.

Drivers:
- EU structural funds (€76B disbursed to Poland 2018-2022)
- German + Czech contracting demand spillover
- Domestic housing shortage (~300k unit deficit)
- Skilled labor pool returning from UK after Brexit
