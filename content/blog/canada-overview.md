Canada is interesting because it sits between US and European models:

- 1.2M total businesses
- 97% have fewer than 100 employees
- Top sectors: retail, construction, professional services
- Provincial variation: BC + Ontario + Alberta dominate
- Resource-sector small businesses prominent in Western provinces
