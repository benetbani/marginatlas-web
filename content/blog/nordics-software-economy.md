Aggregate the SBS data for software development (NACE 62) across the Nordics:

- 🇸🇪 Sweden: 14,200 software firms (1.4 per 1,000 inhabitants)
- 🇫🇮 Finland: 8,400 (1.5 per 1,000)
- 🇩🇰 Denmark: 6,900 (1.2 per 1,000)
- 🇳🇴 Norway: 7,100 (1.3 per 1,000)

For comparison:
- 🇫🇷 France: 0.6 per 1,000
- 🇩🇪 Germany: 0.5 per 1,000
- 🇺🇸 USA: 0.4 per 1,000

The density is striking. Reasons range from:
- High English fluency
- Strong technical universities (KTH, Aalto, DTU)
- Government R&D tax credits
- Small home markets that force early internationalization
- Spotify / Skype / Supercell / Klarna alumni networks producing new founders
