There are roughly 1 hairdresser per 250-400 people in most developed countries. That's remarkably consistent.

What varies wildly is per-firm revenue:
- Switzerland: CHF 220k median
- US: $130k median
- Japan: ¥10M (~$70k) median
- Poland: PLN 280k (~$70k) median
- Greece: €60k median

Labor-intensive, high price elasticity, almost zero scale economies = remarkable global stability.
