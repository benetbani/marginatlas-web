Sources visible on cell pages:

- **US Census Bureau**: Statistics of U.S. Businesses (SUSB), County Business Patterns (CBP), ZIP Business Patterns (ZBP)
- **Eurostat**: Structural Business Statistics across 27 EU + EFTA members
- **Destatis**: Germany's statistical office
- **INSEE**: France
- **e-Stat**: Japan
- **GUS BDL**: Poland
- **IBGE**: Brazil
- **SingStat**: Singapore
- **SSB**: Norway
- **OECD QNA**: quarterly national accounts
- **World Bank Indicators**: macro context

All of these are primary sources. We add value by harmonizing them into one searchable interface.
