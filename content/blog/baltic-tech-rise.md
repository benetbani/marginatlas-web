Skype, Bolt, Wise, Pipedrive, Vinted, all from Estonia or its immediate neighbors.

Baltic software statistics:
- Estonia: 5,200 software firms in a country of 1.3M
- Lithuania: 8,100 in 2.8M
- Latvia: 4,400 in 1.9M

Estonia in particular has 4 software firms per 1,000 inhabitants, 10× the EU average. Foundations:

1. **e-Residency program** lets non-residents incorporate locally
2. **Low corporate tax** (0% on retained earnings)
3. **English-fluent technical workforce**
4. **Skype/TransferWise alumni** founding new firms
5. **Government as a tech customer** (Estonia is the world's most digitally mature government)
