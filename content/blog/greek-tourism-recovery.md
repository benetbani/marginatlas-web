title: "Greek tourism: from crisis to record numbers"
date: "2026-05-05"
excerpt: "Greek tourism employment recovered fully by 2022 after the 2010s debt crisis. The per-firm numbers are smaller than before, though."
author: "Margin Atlas team"
---

Greek tourism is back. Total employment in accommodation + food service (NACE I) hit 348,000 in 2022, surpassing the pre-crisis 2008 peak of 320,000.

But here's the catch: total revenue is at the pre-crisis level, while the number of firms has *increased* 35%. So the average Greek tourism firm is **smaller** than before the crisis.

Median accommodation firm:
- 2008: 8 employees, €420k revenue
- 2022: 5 employees, €280k revenue

The sector recovered through fragmentation — more, smaller operators. Some economists see this as resilience; others as a sign that the recovery is incomplete.
