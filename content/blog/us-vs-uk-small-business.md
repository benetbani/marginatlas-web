A typical US small business and a typical UK small business look more similar than you'd expect — but the differences in specific industries reveal something about each economy's structure.

In retail, US firms median revenue (£780k equivalent) is ~40% higher than UK firms (£560k). In professional services, the gap shrinks: US median around £420k, UK around £390k.

The biggest divergence is in construction. US construction firms are dramatically larger on average — median ~£1.4M vs UK ~£640k. This reflects different regulatory environments and different patterns of subcontracting.

For comparison shoppers: the UK has more single-employee firms (38% of total) than the US (32%). Solo proprietors are the working economy of both countries.

Sources: US Census SUSB 2022, ONS UK Business Activity 2023.
