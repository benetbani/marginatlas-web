Our friendly 80-industry taxonomy is anchored on ISIC Rev.4 sections (the UN standard) and then refined to be browseable by non-technical users.

Every industry has:
- A friendly name (e.g. 'Hairdressers & beauty')
- Bracket examples (e.g. 'barbers, salons, beauty parlors, nail salons')
- Search keywords (lowercase array, type-ahead-friendly)
- A parent sector
- Cross-classification mappings (NAICS-3, NACE-divisions, ISIC-divisions)

This lets us serve 'restaurants' uniformly whether the underlying data is NAICS 722, NACE 56, or e-Stat industry division M.
