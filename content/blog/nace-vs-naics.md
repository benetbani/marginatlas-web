---
title: "NACE vs NAICS — what's the difference?"
date: "2026-05-08"
excerpt: "Europe uses NACE. North America uses NAICS. They differ at the deep levels in important ways."
author: "Margin Atlas team"
---

Both systems use a tree structure with sections (A-U) at the top:

- **NAICS** is North American, has 6-digit codes
- **NACE** is European, has 4-digit codes
- At the 1-2 digit level they're nearly identical
- At 4-6 digit level they diverge — e.g. 'software development' splits differently between IT services and information sub-sectors

Margin Atlas's friendly 80-industry taxonomy bridges both via crosswalk tables, so users see one consistent vocabulary.
