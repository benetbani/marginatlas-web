The city photographs, one per covered city slug as <slug>.jpeg, read into data/cities/images_manifest.json by scripts/build_city_images_manifest.ts. A real photograph only: the grey street map that sat here until 2026-09-11 was ruled out by name ("not the map") and reached the city masthead through this folder.

Since 2026-09-25 the seven United Kingdom cities hold their own photographs, each a public-domain or CC0 file from Wikimedia Commons, found through the Commons API (never a stock host). Where each came from, its licence and its alt text are in data/cities/images_credits.json; a photograph with no line there does not belong here. The country photographs follow the same contract in public/countries/<iso2>.jpeg. The contract: JPEG, 4:3, at least 640x480, under 160,000 bytes.

Since 2026-10-10 (plan docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-10-home-reform, Task 6) thumbs/ holds a 480x360 copy of each photograph, about 25KB, for the home's short rows: written by scripts/build_city_images_manifest.ts --thumbs from the files here (centre cut, JPEG quality 65, with the image library already in node_modules), never by hand and never downloaded. The manifest names each copy and the crop (a CSS object-position) its city's credit gives.
